Tree Surgery

Full Tree Surgery & Felling

Whether it's a single problematic branch or a full felling job, we approach every tree with the same care and precision.

We carry out all types of tree surgery — from straightforward felling on open ground to complex sectional dismantling in confined gardens or near buildings and cables. Every job is assessed individually, and we always look for the least invasive option that solves your problem.

All arisings (timber and brash) are removed and the site left clean. If you'd like the logs keeping for firewood, just say the word.

  • Full tree felling & sectional dismantling
  • Deadwood removal & crown cleaning
  • Work near buildings, fences & cables
  • Site cleared and left spotless
  • TPO and Conservation Area awareness

Crown Work

Crown Reduction & Shaping

A well-done crown reduction should leave the tree looking like it's always been that size. That takes skill — and we've got plenty of it.

Crown reduction involves carefully reducing the overall size of a tree's canopy while maintaining its natural shape and structure. It's often needed when a tree has outgrown its space, is shading the house too heavily, or is becoming a nuisance to neighbours.

We always work to British Standard BS 3998 — the industry benchmark for good tree work. That means no flush cuts, no stubs, and no unnecessary stress to the tree.

  • Crown reduction — height and spread
  • Crown thinning for light & air flow
  • Crown lifting (raising low branches)
  • Formative pruning for younger trees
  • Works to BS 3998 standard throughout

Stump Removal

Stump Grinding & Removal

That old stump in the corner is more than an eyesore — it's a trip hazard, a potential source of fungal disease, and it's taking up space you could use.

We use professional-grade stump grinders to reduce stumps to well below ground level, leaving a clean site you can turf over, pave, or replant. The wood chip produced can be left as mulch or taken away — your choice.

We can handle stumps of any size, in tight spots or on open ground. If you've got several stumps on site, we can often do them all in a single visit at a combined rate.

  • Grinding to 150–300mm below ground
  • All stump sizes — domestic to commercial
  • Wood chip left or removed
  • Ready to turf, pave, or replant
  • Multiple stumps — combined visit rates

Emergency Callouts

Trees don't wait for business hours. Neither do we. When something falls overnight or a storm comes through, we're available around the clock.

Storm damage can happen fast and without warning. A fallen tree blocking your driveway, branches on your roof, a split limb hanging over a neighbour's garden — these things need dealing with quickly and safely.

We respond to emergency callouts across Hull and North Yorkshire, typically same-day. We'll make the area safe, remove what needs removing, and advise on any further work required to prevent it happening again.

Hedges & Boundaries

Hedge Trimming & Shaping

A well-maintained hedge makes the whole garden look cared-for. We'll get yours looking sharp — and keep it that way.

We trim and shape all types of hedging — from small garden privet and box to tall leylandii screens and formal yew boundaries. We work accurately, using lines and levels where needed to get a clean, consistent result.

Timing matters with hedges. We'll always advise on the best time to cut to keep your hedge healthy and avoid nesting bird seasons. Regular maintenance contracts are also available for those who want the job done at the right time, every year, without having to think about it.

  • All species — privet, leylandii, beech, yew & more
  • Sides, tops, formal shaping & reduction
  • All arisings removed and site cleaned up
  • Annual maintenance contracts available
  • Nesting season advice always given
