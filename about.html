About Northern Arborists

We're a small, family-run team — and we think that's exactly what makes us good at what we do.

The Story

Started from the Ground Up — Literally

I'm Harry Beaumont — 24 years old, Hull born and bred, and the person who'll show up on your doorstep when you call us. I got into tree work young, starting out doing clearance and garden jobs as a teenager. What started as weekend graft turned into a proper trade, and I've never looked back.

"If a tree doesn't need to come down, I'll tell you. If there's a cheaper way to solve the problem, you'll hear it from me first."

Ten years of hands-on experience across Hull and North Yorkshire has taught me more than any classroom could. I've worked everything from tight domestic gardens in Cottingham to full commercial site clearances out towards the Wolds — and I've built a reputation on doing it right.

Northern Arborists is my business, my name, and my reputation on the line with every job. That's why I take it seriously. When you get a quote from us, it's me assessing the job. When the crew turns up, I'm there. No middlemen, no subcontracted strangers.

I'm not the oldest arborist in Yorkshire — but I'm one of the hardest working, and the trees don't care how old you are. Ten years in, and I love it as much as I did on day one.

Why It Works

10 Years, Hundreds of Jobs, One Standard

Experience matters in this trade. A decade of working trees across East Yorkshire — big oaks, tight gardens, emergency callouts, awkward stumps — means I've seen pretty much every situation and know how to handle it safely and efficiently.

We carry full public liability insurance on every job. You get an honest quote in writing before any work starts, and the site is left clean when we're done. That's the whole promise — and we keep it.

How We Work

What You Can Expect from Us

We Turn Up When We Say We Will

We know how frustrating it is when a tradesman doesn't show. We confirm every appointment, let you know if anything changes, and arrive on time.

No Hidden Costs

The price we quote is the price you pay. We put everything in writing before any work starts — no surprises on the invoice.

Your Garden, Respected

We treat every property as if it were our own. Protective boards on lawns, careful equipment routing, and a full site clean before we leave.

Straight Talking

We'll tell you what the tree actually needs — not what's most profitable for us. If the tree is fine, we'll say so. If it's dangerous, you'll know exactly why.

Small Team, Personal Service

You'll deal with the same people from first call to job done. No call centres, no subcontractors you've never met — just us.

Better for the Environment

We chip and recycle arisings where possible, avoid unnecessary felling, and always advise on replanting where a tree must come down.
